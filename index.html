Questions

Good to know.

Where do you serve?

Salt Lake City, Park City and the Wasatch Front, including Sandy, Draper, Ogden and Provo. Events farther afield are welcome, and travel is included in your proposal.

How much space is needed?

Roughly a six by six foot area and a standard outlet. We coordinate directly with your venue or planner.

Can the design match our event?

Yes. Layouts, typography, colors and monograms are created for you, and you approve a preview before the evening.

How do guests receive their photos?

By text or email the same night. The host also receives a private gallery with every image.

Is an attendant required?

No. The booth runs on its own. A host is available if you prefer a more attended, white-glove experience.

How do we reserve a date?

Send an inquiry below. After your consultation, a signed agreement and deposit secure your date.
